What rebase actually does

a fake blog post, to prove the widget embeds inline

People talk about git rebase like it picks your commits up and moves them somewhere else. It doesn't — and once you see what it really does, the golden rule stops being folklore and starts being obvious.

Step through it yourself (this is the live widget, iframed right into the post — it starts at the replay step via ?embed&step=7):

Watch the torch: git takes what each of your commits changed, carries it over to main's tip, and builds a brand-new commit there. Your originals never move. They just get abandoned — which is exactly why rebasing commits somebody else already has goes so wrong.
